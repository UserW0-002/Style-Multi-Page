Helpful Baking Websites

Here are five resources for learning techniques, checking measurements, and finding answers to baking questions.

  • King Arthur Baking: Recipe Success Guide

    This guide explains basic measurements, ingredients, and recipe directions.

    It is useful when you want to check the basics before starting a recipe.

  • University of Nebraska–Lincoln: Basic Ingredient Substitutions

    This page lists possible replacements for common ingredients.

    It can help when you are missing an ingredient, but substitutions may change the result.

  • University of Minnesota Extension: Is Flour a Food Safety Concern?

    This article explains food safety concerns involving flour.

    It is useful for learning why uncooked flour and raw batter need careful handling.

  • King Arthur Baking: Ingredient Weight Chart

    This chart lists volume measurements and weights for baking ingredients.

    It is helpful when you want to measure ingredients using a kitchen scale.

  • King Arthur Baking: Baking Guides

    This collection has guides for different baking projects, including cakes, pies, and bread.

    It is useful when you want to practice something new or learn more about a specific type of baking.

Using These Resources

Read your recipe first, then use these websites to check anything you do not understand. You can also review the guide to measuring flour and mixing batter.
