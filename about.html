Why I Made This Site

I made this website to share some basic baking tips. I am taking a baking class and I wanted to use what I'm doing in that for my website project.

I'm still learning so this site's main focus is on simple things that beginners can practice.

What You Can Find Here

The baking guide covers measuring flour and mixing batter. The resources page has websites for checking measurements and learning more.

You don't have to learn everything at once though. Start with one recipe and practice the basics.
