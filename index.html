Introduction to Baking

Baking is the process of cooking food using dry heat, usually inside an oven. This site will give a few tips and resources to help improve your baking skills.

Follow the Recipe

Before trying to bake anything, read the whole recipe. Then gather your ingredients and pay attention to whether anything needs to soften or chill.

Visit the baking guide for two useful skills: measuring flour and mixing batter.

The baking resources page has more information to help when you have questions.

Reflection

  1. The biggest issue I noticed was the navigation links looking weird when the screen was narrow, like on a phone screen.
  2. I used a max-width and automatic margins to keep the content centered. I also used padding and box-sizing to properly space things.
  3. On a wide screen, the navigation links fit next to each other and the content is centered. On a more narrow phone screen the links stack vertically and the cards have less padding.
  4. I learned that testing the different widths can crop content and make it look weird if it is not scaled properly. The meta viewport and content scaling can fix this.
  5. I could improve my navigation and CSS a bit more. Like adding animations and making a mini window with info when you hover over content
