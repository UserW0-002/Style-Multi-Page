Introduction to Baking

Baking is the process of cooking food using dry heat, usually inside an oven. This site will give a few tips and resources to help improve your baking skills.

Follow the Recipe

Before trying to bake anything, read the whole recipe. Then gather your ingredients and pay attention to whether anything needs to soften or chill.

Visit the baking guide for two useful skills: measuring flour and mixing batter.

The baking resources page has more information to help when you have questions.
